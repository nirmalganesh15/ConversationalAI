# Senior Data Engineer, Clinical Data Platform — Helix Therapeutics (Hyderabad, Hybrid)

## About the role
Helix runs multi-country clinical trials and is building a modern clinical data platform that
brings EDC, lab, wearable and real-world data together for biostatistics and safety teams.

## Responsibilities
- Design and build batch and streaming ingestion pipelines for clinical trial data (EDC exports,
  central lab feeds, wearable device streams) using Spark, Airflow and Kafka.
- Model data to CDISC standards (SDTM, ADaM) in collaboration with statistical programmers.
- Build a lakehouse on AWS (S3, Glue, Athena, Redshift) with data quality checks and lineage.
- Implement access controls and audit trails that satisfy GxP and 21 CFR Part 11.
- Work with the pharmacovigilance team to automate adverse-event data feeds.
- Mentor two junior data engineers.

## Requirements
- 6+ years in data engineering, 2+ years with clinical or healthcare data.
- Strong Python and SQL; production experience with Spark and Airflow.
- Experience with streaming (Kafka or Kinesis).
- AWS data services: Glue, Athena, Redshift, Lake Formation.
- Familiarity with CDISC SDTM/ADaM and HL7 FHIR.
- Understanding of GxP, data integrity (ALCOA+) and validation.

## Nice to have
- dbt, Great Expectations or similar data quality tooling.
- Experience with OMOP common data model for real-world evidence.
- Terraform.

# Machine Learning Engineer, NLP — Lumen Bio (Remote, India)

## About the role
Lumen Bio builds NLP models that read biomedical literature and clinical notes to accelerate
target discovery and pharmacovigilance case processing.

## Responsibilities
- Fine-tune and evaluate transformer models (BioBERT, PubMedBERT, open-weight LLMs) for named
  entity recognition, relation extraction and classification on biomedical text.
- Build training and evaluation pipelines with PyTorch and Hugging Face; track experiments in
  MLflow.
- Serve models in production with low latency (Triton, vLLM or BentoML) on Kubernetes.
- Build LLM-based extraction for adverse-event case intake, with human-in-the-loop review.
- Own model monitoring: drift detection, error analysis and retraining triggers.

## Requirements
- 4+ years in ML engineering with NLP focus.
- Strong PyTorch and Hugging Face Transformers; experience fine-tuning (LoRA/QLoRA).
- Experience serving models in production on Kubernetes with GPU scheduling.
- MLOps: MLflow or Weights & Biases, model registry, CI for models.
- Solid evaluation methodology: precision/recall/F1, annotation guidelines, inter-annotator
  agreement.
- Python, SQL; Docker.

## Nice to have
- Biomedical ontologies (MeSH, MedDRA, UMLS, SNOMED CT).
- Publications in NLP or bioinformatics venues.
- Experience with vector search and retrieval-augmented generation.

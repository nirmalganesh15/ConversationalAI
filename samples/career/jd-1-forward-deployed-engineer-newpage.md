# Forward Deployed Engineer — Newpage Solutions (Chennai, Full-time)

## About the role
Newpage Solutions is a global digital health innovation company partnering with pharmaceutical,
biotech and healthcare organisations to build AI and data-driven technologies. Forward Deployed
Engineers treat AI as the substrate of how software gets built. You sit close to clients and
product leaders, reframe vague problems into something concrete, and ship working software
end-to-end — shaping the idea, building it, and standing it up in production yourself.

## What you'll do
- Sit with business or clinical leaders and reframe an idea into something concrete and buildable;
  have a working prototype by the end of the week. Demo live, without a slide deck.
- Lead POCs, innovation sprints and research experiments on emerging AI techniques.
- Build modular backends in Python or TypeScript with clean architecture, OOP, SOLID and DDD.
- Create fullstack applications, APIs, agents and workflows with Next.js, React, FastAPI, Fastify,
  FastMCP and Hono.
- Architect and ship production-grade agentic applications using LangGraph, AutoGen, Claude Agent
  SDK, OpenAI Assistants, or your own orchestration layer.
- Integrate frontier and self-hosted LLMs (Claude, GPT, Gemini, open-weight models) with tools,
  data and external systems through MCP and custom connectors.
- Apply RAG where it helps: vector databases (Pinecone, Chroma, Weaviate, pgvector), hybrid
  retrieval with ElasticSearch or Solr, BM25 + similarity search.
- Design prompt and context engineering frameworks optimising accuracy, cost and latency.
- Use AI-assisted development tools (Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor, Codex) through structured
  workflows, templates and sub-agents — with discipline and review.
- Spin up infra, write evals, wire MCP servers, deploy agents on AWS, Azure, Cloudflare or Vercel
  using Docker, Kubernetes or serverless.
- Treat evals as a first-class discipline with hands-on harnesses.
- Apply TDD, secrets management and rotation, SAST/DAST, structured logging, metrics, tracing and
  automated CI/CD (GitHub Actions, Jenkins). Mentor others on agentic patterns.

## What you bring
- 7+ years of experience, 3+ years building production applications with AI / agentic approaches.
- Hands-on experience with agents — tools wired to a model running multi-step.
- Structured use of AI coding tools with demonstrable workflows, sub-agents and skills.
- Strong Python or TypeScript; OOP, SOLID, 12-factor, microservices; Next.js and FastAPI.
- End-to-end experience with vector databases, retrieval pipelines and eval harnesses.
- Cloud-native deployment on AWS, Azure, Cloudflare or Vercel with Docker, Kubernetes, GitHub
  Actions.
- No-compromise attitude on clean code, TDD, security, observability, performance and cost.
- Deep understanding of how LLMs behave and where they break.
- A recent trail of built things: GitHub, portfolio, side projects, OSS.
- Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science or related field.
